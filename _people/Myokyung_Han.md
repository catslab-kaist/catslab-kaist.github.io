## About Me
Myokyung Han is a Ph.D. student in Digital Humanities and Computational Social Sciences at KAIST. With a solid foundation in computational social sciences, her research focuses on how digital technologies affect the production and consumption of knowledge across various domains. Recently, she has been particularly interested in exploring how the emergence of large language models is transforming the ways people consume and interact with knowledge. To investigate these changes, she aims to integrate theoretical frameworks and analytical methodologies with new big data sources.
