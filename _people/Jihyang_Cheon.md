## About Me
Jihyang Cheon is an M.S. student in the Graduate School of Data Science at KAIST. She studies how knowledge about artificial intelligence varies across academic disciplines and professional domains, using computational analysis of diverse data sources. 
She received her undergraduate degree in IT Engineering with a minor in Convergent Civilization from POSTECH, where she was trained in both the theory and practice of technology, alongside critical reflection on its social implications.
She aims to grow as an interdisciplinary researcher, guided by intellectual curiosity and active communication with researchers from diverse fields.

## My interests

* Information Science
* Science of science
* Sociology of Knowledge
